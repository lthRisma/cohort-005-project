# Cadence

The course platform: learners buy courses, work through their lessons, and can get their money back within limits.

## Language

### Buying

**Purchase**:
A buyer's payment for one course, recording the amount actually paid. A Purchase of a team licence covers seats that other learners claim.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

### Refunds

**Refund**:
The return of the full amount paid for a Purchase, which undoes that Purchase and ends access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback (a dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, not with us), money back

**Refund Request**:
A buyer's ask for a Refund, with a written reason, which is Requested, then Approved or Declined. Only an Approved Refund Request produces a Refund.
_Avoid_: Open refund, pending chargeback, Reversal request, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days, starting on the day of the Purchase, in which a Refund Request may be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window
